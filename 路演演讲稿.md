## 🟢 Tech Deep-Dive (45s) — We're Not Just Eye-Tracking

Here's the hard stuff — we do **triple detection**, not just "is your face there":

1. **Head Orientation** — MediaPipe FaceMesh 468 landmarks, computing yaw/pitch/roll in real time. Head turns past 30° start the distraction timer.
2. **EAR Eye-Closure Detection** — Eye Aspect Ratio algorithm. Eyes closed for 800ms+ triggers it. Normal blinking? Won't fire. We're not dumb.
3. **Head-Tilt & Turn Ratio** — Nose-to-cheek distance ratio. More stable than raw angle calculations, works across different face shapes.

Plus a **Focus Level system** — every minute of focus earns 5 XP, across 5 levels. Level 1 starts with Dream. Level 2 unlocks Sapnap. Level 3? GeorgeNotFound. Want to unlock a new character? **Study harder.** 🎮

Everything runs in the browser. No install, no setup. ESP32 opens a WiFi hotspot, web app talks to it wirelessly.

**We turned self-discipline from a chore into raising a Tamagotchi.**
